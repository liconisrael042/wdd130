La Paz, in Bolivia, is the highest administrative capital in the world, resting on the Andes’ Altiplano plateau at more than 3,500m(11,483ft) above sea level. It stretches to El Alto city in the highlands, with snow-capped, 6,438m(21,122ft)-high Mt. Illimani as its backdrop. It is a pretty and peaceful city.
